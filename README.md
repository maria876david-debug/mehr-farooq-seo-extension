# Mehr Farooq SEO — Quick Links

Local SEO and Google Business Profile quick links and checklist.

The companion extension for mehrfarooq.com, the site of local SEO specialist Mehr Farooq. Run a quick Google Business Profile readiness checklist and jump straight to her services and case-study work. No data is collected.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/mehr-farooq-seo-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://mehrfarooq.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
