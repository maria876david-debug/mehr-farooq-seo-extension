# Your Google Business Profile: The Monthly Check That Keeps Local Customers Finding You

Your Google Business Profile is not a set-and-forget listing. Numbers change, photos go stale, a holiday changes your hours, and a competitor edits what customers see when they search. A monthly check takes fifteen minutes: confirm the phone number and hours are right, add two or three recent photos of real work, answer every new review - good or bad - and check that your services list still matches what you actually sell. Profiles that are touched regularly look alive to Google and to customers; profiles left alone for a year quietly slide down the map results. The businesses that win local search are rarely doing anything clever - they are just the ones still paying attention.
