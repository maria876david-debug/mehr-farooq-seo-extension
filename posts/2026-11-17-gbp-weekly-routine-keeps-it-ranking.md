# Your GBP Is Live — Now What? The Weekly 15-Minute Routine That Keeps It Ranking

Getting your Google Business Profile live is the start, not the finish. A profile that never changes slowly sinks. The routine that keeps it working takes about fifteen minutes a week: check your profile views and calls, answer any new review the same week it arrives, post one short update about your service, and check the questions section for anything unanswered. None of that needs a tool or a budget — it needs consistency. The businesses that show up on Maps are rarely the ones with the fanciest website; they are the ones whose profile is alive every week. If you only have fifteen minutes, spend them on the profile, not on tweaking your homepage. More about how local profiles are built and kept visible: https://mehrfarooq.com
