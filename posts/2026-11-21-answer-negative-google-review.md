# How to Answer a Negative Google Review Without Sounding Defensive

A negative Google review feels personal, but the reply is not written for the reviewer - it is written for the hundreds of people who will read it later. The first rule is speed with calm: reply within a day or two, thank them for the feedback, and name the problem they raised so they feel heard. Never argue, never blame the customer, and never write in anger. If the complaint is fair, say what you will change; if it is a misunderstanding, state the facts once, briefly, and invite them to contact you directly to sort it out. A short, honest reply that owns the fix turns a one-star review into proof that the business listens. The businesses that look best on Google are not the ones with no bad reviews - they are the ones whose replies show they care.
