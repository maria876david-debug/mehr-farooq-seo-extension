# The First Review Is the Hardest: How a New Business Gets Its First Google Reviews

A new business with no Google reviews faces the same question every customer asks: has anyone actually used this business? The first few reviews are the hardest to get, and the only honest way to get them is to ask real customers at the right moment - right after the work is done, while they are still happy with it. Make it easy: send the direct review link, not a search for your business name, and ask once. Never buy reviews, never write your own, and never offer a discount in exchange for one - Google removes them, and customers can tell. One honest review from a real customer is worth more than ten that look staged. A profile with a handful of genuine reviews, answered by the owner, looks like a business that cares. That is the whole job: do good work, ask once, reply to every review.
