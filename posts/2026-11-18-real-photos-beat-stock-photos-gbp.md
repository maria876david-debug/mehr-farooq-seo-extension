# Why Real Photos Beat Stock Photos on Your Business Profile

A Google Business Profile with stock photos looks like every other listing. A profile with real photos of your actual work, your actual shop and your actual team looks like a real business - because it is one. Customers deciding between two similar listings will pick the one they can picture. Real photos also answer the questions people ask before they call: what does the place look like, is it tidy, does it look open for business. You do not need a professional shoot. A clear phone photo in good daylight, taken straight on, does the job. Add new photos whenever the work changes, and retire old ones that no longer show the truth. A profile that looks real gets trusted. A profile that looks borrowed gets skipped.
