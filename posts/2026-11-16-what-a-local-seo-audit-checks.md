# What a Local SEO Audit Actually Checks Before Anything Else

A local SEO audit does not start with keywords. It starts with the Google Business Profile: is the business name, category, hours and services list complete and accurate? Is the map pin in the right place - a pin a few streets off can send real customers to the wrong address.

Then it checks that the name, address and phone number match everywhere the business appears online, because mismatched details confuse Google and customers alike. Reviews come next: not just the star rating, but whether the business answers them. Finally the website itself - does it load fast, does it say clearly what the business does and where, and can a visitor contact the business in one tap? Fix the profile first, then the website. That order saves weeks of wasted effort.
