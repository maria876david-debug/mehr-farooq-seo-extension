# One Page at a Time: Publishing Slowly in the Age of AI

AI can draft a hundred pages before lunch. I still publish one page at a time — and it is a deliberate choice, not a lack of speed.

Every page I publish carries figures someone will act on: a salary, a price, a cost. Each one has to be checked against a named source, signed with my name, and dated. That checking is the work. Generating the words was never the slow part.
